Challenge

Corporate Know Your Customer (KYC) onboarding means collecting dozens of documents, such as risk profiles, tax declarations, and board registers. Compliance teams spend hours chasing missing information and manually checking consistency, and cycles that stretch to weeks create client drop-off and regulatory risk.

Solution

Compliance teams use Mistral Workflows to orchestrate two pipelines: a pre-fill workflow queries external registries to auto-populate documents, and Mistral Small drafts outreach emails listing exactly what is missing. An ingestion workflow then checks returned documents and flags gaps, so the officer iterates until the file is complete.

Result

Pre-fills onboarding documents and asks only for what's missing.
