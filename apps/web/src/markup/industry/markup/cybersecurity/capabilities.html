Vulnerability discovery and remediation.

Move from a suspected flaw to a tested fix. Analyze code, identify vulnerabilities, and reproduce findings in authorized test environments. Generate and test patches so your teams can move from discovery to remediation with evidence.

Security testing and exposure

Find the paths that put critical systems at risk. Assess applications and cloud configurations to identify vulnerabilities and misconfigurations. Validate reachable attack paths and prioritize fixes based on exploitability and the context of your systems and business.

Threat investigation and response

Give analysts a clearer path from alert to action. Build agents that gather evidence across your security tools, connect related events, and reconstruct an incident. Support response decisions with findings analysts can review and actions governed by your policies.

Cybersecurity products

Build differentiated AI into your security stack. Adapt models to your proprietary telemetry, threat intelligence, and detection expertise. Optimize for your product’s accuracy, latency, and cost requirements, and deploy within your customers’ security boundaries.
