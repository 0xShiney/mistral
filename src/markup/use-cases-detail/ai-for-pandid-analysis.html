Challenge

Piping and Instrumentation Diagrams (P&IDs) are the backbone of plant engineering, but the knowledge inside them is locked in PDFs. Engineers manually trace tags, line numbers, and connections across dozens of sheets, which slows troubleshooting, increases risk, and does not scale.

Solution

Engineering teams use Mistral Workflows to read each P&ID, while a Mistral model interprets the diagram, extracts tags, and reconstructs connections as a graph that powers two agents: one searching across diagrams and one answering connectivity questions on a single drawing.

Result

Traces connections across dozens of sheets in one query.
